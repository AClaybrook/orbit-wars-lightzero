How to Read the Board

  • Central Sun (r=10): Any fleet crossing the orange sun is destroyed instantly.
  • Dashed Rings: Inner planets orbit counter-clockwise; outer corner planets are static.
  • Numbers & Dots: Number inside each planet = ships; rim dots = production/turn (+1 to +5).
  • Comets (Tick 50): Four glowing cyan-white comets streak across the map.
  • Fleets (Triangles): Larger fleets fly faster (up to 6 units/turn vs. 1 for single ships).
