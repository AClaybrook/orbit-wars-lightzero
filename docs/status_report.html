Big Win: Exact Physics + Heuristic Dominates 2P

Our rule-based heuristic_agent swept 7 baselines and community bots 100% (including Hugging Face's Orbital Predator v3.0 kvatsa5 and wd_peer) and beat wanderduck's CMA-ES tuned wd_bestv6 3–1 (75%) at ~7–9 ms/turn.

Key Discovery: 4P FFA Blind Spot

Against 3 copies of wd_bestv6 in 4-player FFA, our heuristic went 0–4 (avg rank 3.12). Our TacticalContext currently sums all 3 opponents into one bucket (enemy_total_ships = 75% of board, centroid at the sun), causing bad threat reads and early brawls while other players third-party us.

  • Exact fleet speed formula, swept-pair collision, sun avoidance, & comet spawning
  • Fixed orbital phase offset (init_angle + ω·(step + dt − 1))

  • Vulture sniping neutrals right after enemy weakens/captures them

STEP 1 — HIGHEST IMMEDIATE ROI

Fix 4P FFA Awareness Before Training

Target: lift 4P FFA vs. 3× wd_bestv6 from 0% (rank 3.12) to >30% (rank <2.3) while keeping >75% in 2P.

  • Per-opponent tracking: measure our strength vs. the leader and nearest neighbor (not the sum of 3 opponents).
  • 4P anti-brawl & third-partying: avoid early 1v1 slugfests; detect enemy-vs-enemy battles and snipe weakened winners.
  • Longer timeline (45–60 ticks) + stack pooling: exploit Orbit Wars' log(ships)^1.5 fleet speed scaling.
PATH A — LIGHTZERO

Full-Turn Stances + AlphaZero / Gumbel MZ

Upgrade macro-actions to 8–12 complete multi-order stances, add 4P support to OrbitWarsLightZeroEnv, and train vs. wd_bestv6 + self-play.

PATH B — RECOMMENDED BEST ROI

Neural Value Net + Candidate Rollouts

Generate 6–10 diverse full-turn plans each tick, roll out 15–20 ticks in FastOrbitSim (<120 ms), and train a neural V(s) on 2P+4P games to choose the best future board.

PATH C — ADVANCED / GPU

Entity Transformer PPO Self-Play

Move off the 16×16 grid to a Set Transformer directly over planet & fleet entities with a Cython/Rust simulator for millions of self-play games.
