Knob 1: DAgger on Lost Games

Played 16 student games (10× 2P vs. #2 Hober, 6× 4P vs. 3× subham) querying Hober Malloc as on-policy oracle (+1,228 states). Diagnosed 69.1% turn-level divergence; 9 resumed epochs lifted val_all 72.9% → 76.9%, val_vsign 95.4% → 97.3%, and flipped 4P vs. 3× subham from 1–3 → 4–0.

Knob 2: Self-Play PPO + Anchor-KL

5 iterations (40 mixed 2P/4P league episodes in FastOrbitSim, 5,635 states) with per-planet clipped PPO + Anchor-KL (kl=0.06). Beat both PT-v1 (5–1) and its own DAgger v2 anchor (5–1) — proving improvement beyond teacher imitation — and swept 3× subham 4–0 in 130.8 ticks.

AlphaZero Value-Guided Lookahead

# 1. DAgger on-policy oracle collection + resume training (PlanetTransformer v2)
python scripts/train_planet_transformer.py --dataset-cache pt_dataset.npz --save-cache pt_dataset_dagger.npz \
  --dagger-checkpoint checkpoints/planet_transformer_v1_backup.pt --dagger-2p 10 --dagger-4p 6 --collect-only
python scripts/train_planet_transformer.py --dataset-cache pt_dataset_dagger.npz \
  --resume checkpoints/planet_transformer_v1_backup.pt --epochs 9 --lr 7e-4 --out checkpoints/planet_transformer_v2.pt

# 2. Self-Play PPO + Anchor-KL RL fine-tuning (PlanetTransformer v2+RL)
python scripts/rl_finetune_transformer.py --init checkpoints/planet_transformer_v2.pt \
  --out checkpoints/planet_transformer_v2_rl.pt --iterations 5 --episodes-per-iter 8 --kl-coef 0.06

# 3. Evaluate pure policy or AlphaZero-style value-guided lookahead search
