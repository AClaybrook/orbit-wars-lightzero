1. Critical Defense Deficit Bug Fix (physics.py)

simulate_planet_future previously negated post-capture enemy ships across all 36 future ticks, inflating a 7-ship defense deficit to 112+ ships!

  • Fixed min_owner_ships to track true unflipped owner defense balance
  • Added atomic in-time defense (never trickle into unsaveable fights)
  • Fixed double-retake bug (fut.final_owner == player) & last-tick-only evacuation

2. 4P FFA Diplomacy & Unified ROI (macro_actions.py)

Eliminated the 4P blind spot where 3 enemies were summed into one bucket with centroid at the sun.

  • Per-opponent ship/prod/planet tracking (leader_enemy_id, is_ffa)
  • Static vs. orbiting durability scoring + comet discounting + quadrant safety
  • Unified neutral + enemy ROI allocator with 2.15× opponent-elimination bonus

3. Fleet Speed & Anti-Ping-Pong (heuristic_bot.py)

Diagnostics showed v1 kept 75% of its army in flight as slow 5-ship droplets.

  • Tightly synchronized multi-source captures (max 3–4 tick arrival spread)
  • Short-hop (≤6 tick) pooled backline consolidation (≥24 ships, no ping-pong)
  • Top-neutral opening reservation patience (hold 1–4 ticks for high-prod neutrals)

4. Active-Opponent MCTS + LightZero NN (mcts_agent.py)

Fixed MCTS's single-action bottleneck, 2P-only hardcoding, and frozen-opponent rollout bias.

  • Native 2P & 4P FastOrbitSim rollouts with active mid-ply opponent defense
  • Deduplicated curated full-turn stances anchored by BALANCED_MACRO
  • Warm-started LightZero AlphaZeroModel (76.7% val sign acc) + torch.set_num_threads(1)
