Why Impulsive All-In Launches Snowball Against #2

1. Self-Inflicted Garrison Vacuums (94 Errors, 44.5%)

2. Premature Solo Bounces (29 Errors, 13.7%)

Against weaker bots, aggressive all-in pokes steal planets immediately. Against #2's tighter defense, solo pokes bounce cleanly — feeding ship efficiency directly to #2 instead of accumulating for a multi-planet hammer blow.

3. Training Distribution Shift

Even with Soft-KD, our uniform-epoch sampling puts equal weight on trivial early/late states while the game is actually decided in the razor-thin t = 25–65 HOLD-vs-LAUNCH boundary.
