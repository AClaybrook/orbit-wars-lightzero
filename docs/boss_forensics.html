Key Orbit Wars Physics Lesson Discovered in Gen 9

Why did hard-coded neutral capping lose 2–4 against PT-Gen8 while #2 Hober Malloc sends 100% All-In yet has lower overkill?

Fleet Speed Scales Nonlinearly With Ship Count

In Orbit Wars, speed = 1 + 5 · (ln(ships) / ln(1000))1.5. Capping a 50-ship planet down to 12 ships slows the fleet dramatically, losing race-to-neutral timing and delaying planet production ticks.

True Mechanism: #2 Hober Malloc achieves low neutral overkill by launching earlier when a planet naturally holds just enough ships — which is why neural timing (Soft-KD) beat decoder caps cleanly.

Small-Planet Bias Remains Ruled Out

Both PT-Gen8 (3.28) and PT-Gen9 (3.29) target slightly higher average neutral production than #2 Hober Malloc (3.19), with 27 high-prod (≥4) neutral captures across 6 seeds.
